A private trading diary that separates core holdings from trading cycles and lets you match a recorded sale to specific purchase lots.

What is included

  • A name-only local profile, with no passwords, brokerage login or cloud account.
  • CSV and Excel imports, with column detection, manual column mapping, preview and confirmation. PDF text preview is local, but arbitrary PDF layouts are not automatically converted to trades.
  • Purchase lots classified as Core or Trading, a holdings dashboard, manual stock prices, and manually entered cash balance.
  • Record a sale made in your broker account. Allocate the sold quantity across chosen lots, fill the cheapest lots first, or choose FIFO.
  • Completed lot cycles showing gross result, proportional recorded charges, net result, dates and holding duration. Independent FIFO comparison.
  • Transaction history, decision notes, monthly cycle summaries, and a holdings snapshot reconciliation.
  • Validated JSON backup import/export, transaction CSV export, last-transaction undo, and explicit confirmation before clearing data.
  • An Android-installable PWA with a distinct icon and offline app cache.

Which Groww documents to use

Start with equity Order / Trade history for every relevant period, ideally from the first purchase. In Groww, use Profile → Reports → Transactions, choose Stocks Order History and export Excel or CSV. Next upload a Stock Holding Statement with stock and quantity; broker average price is optional. A snapshot checks quantities; it does not invent historical trades. Contract notes help you enter actual charges when they are absent from order history.

A P&L summary or balance statement alone is insufficient to reconstruct purchase lots. The app explains missing history and rejects imports that would oversell a holding. If older purchases are unavailable, explicitly record an opening lot with its known quantity and cost, using the note “opening balance”. Use matching stock identifiers across reports and manual entries.

The lot example

Event Quantity Price Purpose
Earlier buy 10 ₹1,300 Core
Later buy 10 ₹1,250 Trading
Sale allocated to later buy 10 ₹1,270 Cycle closed
Diary realised: +₹200. FIFO realised: −₹300.

The diary retains the ₹1,300 core lot; FIFO retains the ₹1,250 lot. At ₹1,270, total realised plus unrealised is −₹100 in either view, before charges. Lot attribution cannot create wealth or alter broker or tax records.

First-use flow

  1. Open Lotbook and enter your name. You can explore synthetic demo data or skip uploads and start manually.
  2. Choose the right statement type, review parsed rows, choose Core/Trading for each purchase, and confirm. Separate identical fills are retained on first import; repeat uploads are matched by execution identity or occurrence count and reviewed before saving.
  3. Tap a stock for every purchase lot and its remaining balance. Update the manually entered price when needed.
  4. After selling through Groww, choose Record sale, enter the date, quantity, price and fees, then allocate the full quantity across available lots. Confirm only when the preview is correct.
  5. Review Cycles for realised results and the Diary for the transaction trail. Export a backup before changing devices or clearing browser storage.

Privacy and storage

Your name, parsed transactions, quotes, snapshots and notes are stored in this browser’s local storage. Original statements are read in memory and discarded after parsing. Financial records are never uploaded to GitHub, an API, analytics, or an AI service. GitHub Pages receives normal requests for app files. The app uses locally bundled libraries, icons and fonts.

This is not encrypted cloud storage or a secure sign-in system. A person using the same unlocked browser can access the diary. Incognito sessions, browser clearing, storage eviction or device loss can erase records. Keep JSON backups privately; those exports contain your financial information. Each device has a separate diary unless you transfer a backup.

Android and offline use

Open the hosted URL in Android Chrome. Use Install app when offered, or Chrome’s menu → Add to Home screen. A standalone Lotbook icon opens the app. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Browser prompts vary; in-app browsers may not offer installation. Offline use starts after the initial app cache finishes downloading online.

Scope and known limits

Equity long positions only. No broker connection, live quotes, actual order execution, dividend accounting, tax filing, corporate actions, transfer reconstruction, mutual funds, F&O, margin or short-selling support. Imported sales initially use FIFO until specifically recorded with a diary lot allocation. Past sales can be rematched only to lots available before that sale; the whole diary is replayed before saving. Same-day history uses execution time when every trade has one, otherwise it preserves report order. Missing fees remain unknown, not zero. The current Groww Order History and Holding Statement formats were tested locally; P&L and Capital Gains summaries are rejected as trade sources.

Files are limited to 15 MB and parsed previews to 20,000 rows. Unrecognised rows are flagged. JSON restores are validated before replacing records. The journal is for understanding your trading decisions; official broker statements remain your reference for settlement and tax reporting.

How it is verified

The release is checked with accounting and importer tests, a production build, mobile-width layout checks, and browser flows for onboarding, purchases, cost corrections, past-sale lot matching and persistence. A supplied local Groww report set was parsed without uploading or committing it. Execution history replayed cleanly, current positions reconciled, and a real missing-history difference was visibly reported.
